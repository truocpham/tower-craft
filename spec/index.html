Tower Craft

A one-tap stacking game made for children under 10 to play on an iPad Air at home. A crane swings a house over a tower. Tap to drop it and keep building.

Play the prototype

Summary

Product

A private game for your own family. It is not published to the App Store or TestFlight. No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no network.

Core loop

Swing, tap, drop, slice, stack. Score is the number of houses stacked. One miss ends the run, as in the classic version.

Engine

Godot 4, 2D, GDScript. Developed on Windows, Linux or macOS. The iPad build is made on your MacBook Pro (M3 Pro).

Target

Your iPad Air, in portrait and landscape. A Mac desktop build is kept for testing. iPhone is optional later.

Decisions locked. 2D, not 3D. The art in the approved prototype is the final art. No store release. One miss ends the run. The only personal touch is the child's name. The game is tailored to children under 10, so the details in "Kid tailoring" matter more than commercial features.

Gameplay

Round flow

  1. A house hangs from a crane hook and swings left and right over the tower on a rope.
  2. The child taps anywhere. The house releases and falls straight down.
  3. On landing, the game compares the house to the top of the tower and resolves one of three outcomes.
  4. The camera scrolls up, the next house appears and the swing continues slightly faster.

Landing outcomes

OutcomeCondition (Easy default)Result
PerfectOffset under 12% of a house width (Easy)House snaps to the tower centre and keeps its full width. Streak +1. A cheerful "Perfect!" pop-up shows.
PartialSome overlap with the top houseThe house is trimmed to the overlap. The overhang breaks off and tumbles away. Streak resets to 0.
MissNo overlap with the top houseThe whole house tumbles away with a friendly "Oops!" and the run ends. The tower stays on screen while the result appears.

Regrow

From the second perfect in a row on Easy (third on Normal), each perfect landing makes that house 15% wider on Easy (12% on Normal), up to the full starting width. A narrow tower can always recover.

Art and audio

Art: the prototype is final

  • The houses, logo, sky, clouds and ground strip from prototype v2 are the shipped art. They are drawn with code in the prototype, so the Godot version redraws the same shapes into sprite images at high resolution, so they stay sharp on iPad screens.
  • Sky gradient shifts with altitude: day blue, clear afternoon, dusk, then space.
  • Drifting cloud layers and a hazard-stripe ground strip that scrolls away as the tower grows.
  • New for kids: confetti on milestones, a smiling sun in the corner that reacts to perfect landings, and soft bounce animation on every landing.

Original art only. All art is drawn for this game and must stay free of other games' artwork and logos.

Audio and feedback

  • Soft drop sound, landing thud and a rising-pitch chime on each perfect in a streak.
  • A calm looping music track, with separate music and sound toggles in settings.
  • Optional voice cheers (for example "Nice!" and "Wow!") for children who do not read yet.
  • iPad haptics are not available, so feedback is audio and visual only.

Tech and build

Engine and language

Godot 4 with GDScript. Scenes for profile pick, title, game, run complete, styles and parent area. All tuning in one resource file.

Development hosts

Windows, Linux and macOS all run the same project. Logic tests run on any of them.

iPad install

Export the Godot project to Xcode on your MacBook Pro, sign it with your own Apple ID and install it on the iPad Air by cable or Wi-Fi. The iPad needs Developer Mode turned on. At M2 we check that your Xcode version supports your iPadOS version.

No store release

No App Store, no TestFlight, no store listing, screenshots or review. A paid Apple Developer account is optional.

What a free Apple ID means

  • The installed app expires after 7 days. To keep it playable, reinstall from Xcode on the Mac about once a week.
  • A free account allows only a few apps installed this way at a time, which is enough for this game.
  • The paid Apple Developer account ($99 per year) extends the install to one year and allows private TestFlight builds for family. It is a convenience, not a requirement.

Project layout (proposed)

  • /game Godot project: scenes, scripts, resources, art and audio.
  • /game/data/tuning.tres the parameters from the tables above.
  • /prototype the approved browser prototype, kept as the feel reference.
  • /spec this document.

Testing

  • Unit tests for the landing maths: perfect, partial, miss and regrow, at several screen sizes.
  • A deterministic mode with a fixed swing phase for repeatable tests.
  • Play-testing with the children on the real iPad after each milestone. Their feedback sets the final Easy and Normal numbers.

  • App Store or TestFlight release, store listing, screenshots and review.
